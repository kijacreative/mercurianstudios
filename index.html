04built with margin

The method

Built with margin.

Margin is an engineering word. It's the room you design in on purpose, so when something goes wrong — and it will — you're not out of runway. Most productions run with none.

01

Trade study

Agree up front on what we're optimizing for and what we're willing to trade.

02

Build plan

One document everyone works from. Schedule, budget, crew, gear, contingencies.

03

Debrief

After wrap, write down what we learned so the next one costs less.
