03built with margin

How it runs

Built with margin.

Margin is an engineering word. It's the room you design in on purpose, so when something goes wrong — and it will — you're not out of runway. Most productions run with none. Every job I take runs on the same three beats.

01

Trade study

Agree up front on what we're optimizing for and what we're willing to trade.

02

One document everyone works from. Schedule, budget, crew, gear, contingencies.

03

Debrief
